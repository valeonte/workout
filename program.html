Training programme

Context: post-meniscus repair rehab Schedule: Mon / Wed / Thu + Fri basketball Session length: 45–60 min

Monday

Full gym · ~60 min
ExerciseSets × repsNotes
Rowing5 minWarm-up, steady pace.
Squat → Overhead Press3 × 8Full depth, heels raised. One continuous rep.
Leg Press (both legs)3 × 10Stop at approx. 90° knee flexion. Seat set so deeper range is not physically available.
Deadlift → Upright Row3 × 8One continuous rep.
Split Squat3 × 8 / legDepth to pain-free tolerance on the operated side.
Assisted Pull-Up3 × 8Assist set so the final rep is hard but controlled.
Machine Chest Press3 × 10
Seated Hamstring Curl (both legs)3 × 12Unrestricted.
Seated Calf Raise3 × 15Knees bent — soleus emphasis, as prescribed. Slow concentric and eccentric.
Cable Bicep Curl3 × 12
Plank3 × 40s

If equipment is occupied: leg press → hack squat (same 90° limit) or goblet squat · assisted pull-up → lat pulldown or band-assisted · machine chest press → pec deck or dumbbell bench · seated calf raise → bodyweight bent-knee soleus raise.

Work gym · ~45 min
ExerciseSets × repsNotes
